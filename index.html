How it works

  1. 1

    Connect a wallet that holds an Agent ID

    Only Agent ID holders can mint. The contract checks the registry on-chain at mint time.

  2. 2

    Mint your free badge

    One badge per address. It is soulbound: it can never be transferred or sold, only burned by you.

  3. 3

    Check in daily to grow your streak

    One check-in per UTC day. Consecutive days grow the streak; a missed day resets it to 1.

The fine print

  • Free. Minting costs nothing but the network gas fee. The contract can never hold funds.
  • Soulbound. The badge cannot be transferred, sold, or approved. Burn it any time to free your address to mint again.
  • No rewards. The badge pays nothing and promises nothing. It is a participation marker, not an investment.
  • No owner. The contract has no admin, no pause switch, and no way to change the rules after deployment.
  • Community project. Not affiliated with any wallet, exchange, or chain operator.
